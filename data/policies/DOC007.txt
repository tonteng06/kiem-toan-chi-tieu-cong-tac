title: Expense Exception Policy
version: 1.0
date: 2026-02-01
owner: Finance

An expense that exceeds a policy limit may still be reviewed as an exception.

The employee must provide a reason for the exception request.

An over-limit expense must not be automatically approved.

The expense must be escalated to a manager for review.

After reviewing the evidence, the manager may approve or reject the exception.
