title: Travel Expense Policy
version: 1.0
date: 2025-01-01
owner: Finance

Employees must submit travel expense claims within 15 calendar days after returning from a business trip.

Hotel, meal, and transportation expenses may be reimbursed when they are related to approved business travel.

Receipts should be provided where required by company policy.

Claims with missing required receipts may be rejected or sent for further review.
