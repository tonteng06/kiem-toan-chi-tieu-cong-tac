title: International Travel Policy
version: 1.0
date: 2026-02-01
owner: Finance

International business travel requires prior manager approval.

International meal reimbursement follows the limits defined in the Meal Allowance Policy.

International hotel reimbursement limits are destination-specific and must be defined by an applicable destination policy.

If no destination-specific hotel policy is available, the system must not invent a hotel limit.

When the available policy information is insufficient to determine the correct reimbursement limit, the claim must be sent for manual review.
