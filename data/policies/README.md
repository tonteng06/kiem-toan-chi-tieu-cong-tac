# Policy Corpus Provenance

DOC001-DOC008 form a synthetic travel-expense policy corpus created specifically for this academic project.

The documents are not copied from, and do not represent, the policies of a real company.

The corpus was designed around the Travel Expense Audit Agent scenario in the project brief. It includes policy information for:

- travel expense submission deadlines;
- hotel reimbursement limits;
- meal allowances;
- ground transportation;
- receipt requirements;
- expense exceptions and escalation;
- international travel;
- policy versioning.

The corpus also intentionally leaves some topics undefined so that the system can be evaluated on no-answer cases.

Each policy document includes the required metadata:

- `doc_id`
- `title`
- `version`
- `date`
- `owner`

The corpus is used only for academic development and evaluation of the RAG and Agent pipeline.
