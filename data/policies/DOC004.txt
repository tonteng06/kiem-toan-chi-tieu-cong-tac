title: Meal Allowance Policy
version: 1.0
date: 2026-02-01
owner: Finance

The domestic meal allowance is limited to 500,000 VND per employee per day.

The international meal allowance is limited to 1,000,000 VND per employee per day.

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable.

Meal expenses above the applicable daily allowance require manager review.
