title: Hotel Reimbursement Limits
version: 1.0
date: 2026-02-01
owner: Finance

The maximum reimbursable hotel rate for domestic business travel is:

Ho Chi Minh City: 2,000,000 VND per night.
Hanoi: 2,000,000 VND per night.
Da Nang: 1,500,000 VND per night.
Other domestic locations: 1,200,000 VND per night.

A hotel expense above the applicable limit cannot be automatically approved.

Expenses above the hotel limit must be escalated for manager review.
