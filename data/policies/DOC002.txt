This policy supersedes Travel Expense Policy version 1.0.

Employees must submit travel expense claims within 10 calendar days after returning from a business trip.

Late submissions require manager approval before reimbursement can proceed.

An individual expense of 500,000 VND or more must have a receipt.

When multiple versions of this policy exist, the newest effective version must be used.
