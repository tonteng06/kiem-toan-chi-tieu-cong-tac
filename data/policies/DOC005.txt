Taxi and ride-hailing expenses are reimbursable when they are for an approved business purpose.

The employee must record the business purpose of the transportation expense.

An individual taxi or ride-hailing expense of 500,000 VND or more requires a receipt.

Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable unless prior approval was obtained.
