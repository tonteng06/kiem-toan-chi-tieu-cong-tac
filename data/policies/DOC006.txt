doc_id: DOC006
title: Receipt Requirements
version: 1.0
date: 2026-02-01
owner: Finance

A receipt is required for every individual expense of 500,000 VND or more.

Expenses below 500,000 VND may be submitted without a receipt unless another policy requires one.

If a required receipt is missing, the expense cannot be automatically approved.

A manager may approve an exception after reviewing the supporting explanation.

The system must record when a required receipt is missing.
