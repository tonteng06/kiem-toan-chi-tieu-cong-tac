### Completed (plan Day 2: state store + calculate_claim)

- `src/agent/state_store.py`: JSON-backed claim store, the only place that reads or writes claim state.
  - `StateStore.all()`, `get(claim_id)`, `update_status(claim_id, new_status, reason)`.
  - `update_status` accepts a `ClaimStatus` or a string. It raises `StateStoreError` for an unknown status, an empty reason, a missing claim, or a transition that `TRANSITIONS` does not allow (e.g. re-deciding APPROVED/REJECTED).
  - Sets `decision_reason` and `updated_at` (UTC+7, ISO, seconds) on every change.
  - Atomic write (write `.tmp`, then `replace`), so a crash never leaves a half-written file.
  - Errors are raised as exceptions here; the tool layer (Day 3) will turn them into `Error: ...` strings.
- `src/agent/business_rules.py`: `calculate_claim(claim)` is read-only and deterministic.
  - Returns `claim_id`, `summary`, `total`, `totals`, `violations`, `missing_data`, `recommended_status`.
  - Every issue carries a code, a message, an outcome and an evidence ref (e.g. `DOC003@v1.0`, same format as TV3's draft `DOC003@v1.2`).
  - Rules: alcohol not reimbursable (DOC004), hotel per-night limit by city (DOC003), meal per-day limit (DOC004), receipt required from 500,000 VND (DOC006), submission within 10 days (DOC002 v2.0, supersedes DOC001 v1.0 = 15 days), international trip needs prior approval and has no invented hotel limit (DOC008).
  - Outcome priority when several apply: REJECTED > NEED_MORE_INFO > ESCALATED > APPROVED.
- Tests: `tests/test_state_store.py` (5 tests, run on a copy of the sample data in `tmp_path`) and `tests/test_business_rules.py` (10 tests: 7 sample outcomes, totals, issue codes and evidence, read-only check).

### Design decisions

- Over a limit means ESCALATED, not REJECTED, because the policy sends exceptions to a human reviewer (DOC007).
- International claims never get a made-up hotel limit; they are escalated for manual review.
- `calculate_claim` only recommends; changing state happens only through `update_status`, so the check and the side effect stay separate.
- Limits are constants at the top of `business_rules.py`, each with its policy reference.
